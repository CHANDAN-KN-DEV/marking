PRIMARY IMPROVEMENT OPPORTUNITY

Consistent Customer Acquisition

Create an additional, manageable channel for attracting new business enquiries without requiring complicated new workflows or additional staffing.

Diagnostic Findings Supporting This Priority:

  • The owner explicitly identified finding new customers consistently as the primary operational challenge.
  • Existing construction relationships provide orders, but reaching new buyers outside those networks remains difficult.
  • Marketing activities are currently sporadic (restricted to seasonal or festival greetings).
  • The business targets verified contractors, builders, architects, and tile installers.
Strategic Interpretation: The central obstacle is not a complete absence of digital capabilities; it is the absence of a systematic, manageable channel to acquire business buyers beyond existing relationships.
